ABOUT TECHNOVANZA

01 / INSTITUTION

ABOUT THE COLLEGE

CHRIST ACADEMY INSTITUTE FOR ADVANCED STUDIES

Christ Academy Institute for Advanced Studies (CAIAS), affiliated to Bangalore University, is a vibrant institution committed to academic excellence, innovation, and holistic student development. With modern infrastructure, advanced laboratories, digital learning facilities, and a strong focus on research and experiential learning, CAIAS empowers students to become future-ready professionals.

Through dedicated faculty, mentoring, industry exposure, and diverse co-curricular activities, the institution nurtures creativity, leadership, and ethical values.

CAIAS — Empowering minds, inspiring innovation, shaping the future.

02 / DISCIPLINE

ABOUT THE DEPARTMENT

DEPARTMENT OF COMPUTER SCIENCE AND APPLICATIONS

The Department of Computer Science and Applications at CAIAS is dedicated to developing skilled IT professionals through a blend of academic excellence, practical learning, and industry exposure.

With programmes including BCA, BCA in AI & ML, BCA in Data Science, and MCA, the department focuses on emerging technologies such as Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Data Science, and Cloud Computing.

Through workshops, internships, coding competitions, research projects, and industry collaborations, students are prepared to become innovative and responsible technology professionals.

03 / COMMUNITY

ABOUT THE INT-ELEGANCE CLUB

INT-ELEGANCE

Think Intelligently. Build Elegantly.

Int-Elegance, the IT Club of CAIAS, is a dynamic platform that brings together technology, creativity, and innovation. Through coding challenges, technical quizzes, workshops, paper presentations, and technical fests, the club encourages students to develop technical skills, critical thinking, teamwork, and leadership.

Int-Elegance — Where ideas meet innovation and technology meets purpose.

04 / EXPERIENCE

ABOUT TECHNOVANZA

WHERE IDEAS IGNITE. INNOVATION TAKES OVER.

The Int-Elegance Club, in association with the Department of Computer Science and Applications, proudly presents TECHNOVANZA 2026 — a One Day IT Fest celebrating technology, creativity, and innovation.

From coding challenges and tech quizzes to Ideathons, TECHNOVANZA 2026 offers a vibrant platform for students to showcase their skills, challenge their limits, and transform ideas into possibilities.

More than a competition, TECHNOVANZA is a celebration of collaboration, problem-solving, and the spirit of innovation.

Think Beyond. Code Beyond. Create Beyond.
Your ideas. Your skills. Your moment.

OUR VISION

To build an inclusive and forward-thinking student community that nurtures curiosity, creativity, and technical excellence in technologies, empowering members to become agile learners, collaborative problem-solvers, and responsible contributors to the evolving digital world.

“Where Ideas Meet Innovation.”
Participation Rules

GENERAL GUIDELINES OF THE EVENT

Registration & Participation

  • Registration is mandatory and will be accepted on a first-come, first-served basis.
  • Participants may register online or offline.
  • On-the-spot registration begins at 8:00 AM on the event day, subject to availability.
  • Participants must report at least 15 minutes before the event begins.
  • A valid college ID card is required for participation.
  • For team events, all members must be registered.
  • In case of overlapping events, participants must choose one—no adjustments will be made.

Code of Conduct

  • Participants must maintain discipline and decorum throughout the event.
  • Misconduct, disrespect, or use of offensive language will result in disqualification.
  • Malpractice or cheating in any form is strictly prohibited.

Rules & Instructions

  • Event-specific instructions will be given by coordinators before the start of each event.
  • The judges’ decisions are final and binding.

Certificates & Contact

  • All participants will receive E-certificates.
  • Winners will be awarded certificates, trophies, or prizes as applicable.
  • For any queries or registration issues, please contact the designated event coordinator or visit the help desk at the venue.

Queries & Contact

For any questions regarding the event or registration, please contact the assigned event coordinators. Their details are provided on the last page of the brochure. A help desk will also be available at the venue for on-the-spot assistance.
