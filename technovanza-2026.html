About the college

CAIAS

Christ Academy Institute for Advanced Studies

Affiliated to Bangalore University, CAIAS is a vibrant institution committed to academic excellence, innovation, and holistic student development. With modern infrastructure, advanced laboratories, digital learning facilities, and a strong focus on research and experiential learning, it empowers students to become future-ready professionals.

Through dedicated faculty, mentoring, industry exposure, and diverse co-curricular activities, the institution nurtures creativity, leadership, and ethical values.

Empowering minds, inspiring innovation, shaping the future.

About the department

Computer Science and Applications

The department develops skilled IT professionals through academic excellence, practical learning, and industry exposure. Programmes include BCA, BCA in AI & ML, BCA in Data Science, and MCA, with a focus on Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Data Science, and Cloud Computing.

Through workshops, internships, coding competitions, research projects, and industry collaborations, students are prepared to become innovative and responsible technology professionals.

About the club

Int-Elegance

Think Intelligently. Build Elegantly.

Int-Elegance, the IT Club of CAIAS, brings together technology, creativity, and innovation. Through coding challenges, technical quizzes, workshops, paper presentations, and technical fests, the club builds technical skills, critical thinking, teamwork, and leadership.

Where ideas meet innovation and technology meets purpose.

About the fest

Where ideas ignite

Innovation takes over.

The Int-Elegance Club, with the Department of Computer Science and Applications, presents TECHNOVANZA 2026: a one day IT fest celebrating technology, creativity, and innovation. From coding challenges and tech quizzes to Ideathons, it is a platform to show your skills, test your limits, and turn ideas into possibilities.

More than a competition, it is a celebration of collaboration, problem-solving, and the spirit of innovation.

Think Beyond. Code Beyond. Create Beyond.

Participation rules

General guidelines

Registration & participation

  • Registration is mandatory and accepted on a first-come, first-served basis.
  • Participants may register online or offline.
  • On-the-spot registration begins at 8:00 AM on the event day, subject to availability.
  • Participants must report at least 15 minutes before the event begins.
  • A valid college ID card is required for participation.
  • For team events, all members must be registered.
  • In case of overlapping events, participants must choose one. No adjustments will be made.

Code of conduct

  • Participants must maintain discipline and decorum throughout the event.
  • Misconduct, disrespect, or offensive language will result in disqualification.
  • Malpractice or cheating in any form is strictly prohibited.

Rules & instructions

  • Event-specific instructions will be given by coordinators before each event.
  • The judges’ decisions are final and binding.

Certificates

  • All participants will receive e-certificates.
  • Winners will be awarded certificates, trophies, or prizes as applicable.

Queries & contact

  • For questions about the event or registration, contact the assigned event coordinators. A help desk will also be available at the venue for on-the-spot assistance.
